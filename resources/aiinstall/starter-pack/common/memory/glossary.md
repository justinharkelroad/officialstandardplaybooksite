# Glossary

> Pre-loaded with common agency terms. In Phase 7: cut what you do not use, fix any definition your agency uses differently, and add your own language at the bottom. Every term your team uses that an outsider would not know belongs here.

## Book and production

- **PIF**: Policies in force. The count of active policies on the book.
- **DWP**: Direct written premium. Total premium written, before anything comes out.
- **Book of business**: All active policies and households the agency holds.
- **Household**: All policies belonging to one client family, counted together.
- **Monoline**: A household with only one policy type (e.g. auto only). Prime cross-sell target.
- **Bundle / multiline**: A household with two or more lines (e.g. auto + home). Stickier and more profitable.
- **Bundle rate**: Percentage of households with more than one line.
- **Cross-sell**: Selling an additional line to an existing client.
- **Quote-to-bind ratio**: Of quotes given, how many became policies.
- **Rewrite**: Re-issuing an existing policy, often to fix rate or coverage structure.
- **Endorsement**: A mid-term change to an existing policy.

## Retention and money

- **Retention**: Percentage of the book that renews and stays. The number that quietly runs the agency.
- **Win-back**: A campaign to recover clients who left.
- **Cancellation save**: Catching and reversing a pending cancellation (often a missed payment).
- **Renewal increase**: The premium jump a client sees at renewal; drives the hardest client conversations of the year.
- **Chargeback**: Commission clawed back when a policy cancels early.
- **Loss ratio**: Claims paid versus premium collected. Carriers watch it; captives get measured on it.
- **Trailing twelve**: Any number measured over the last 12 months.

## People and structure

- **EA**: Exclusive agent. An agent contracted to one captive carrier (the Allstate model).
- **Captive**: An agency writing for a single carrier (Allstate, State Farm, Farmers).
- **Independent**: An agency with appointments across multiple carriers.
- **Carrier appointment**: An independent agency's authorization to write for a specific carrier.
- **LSP**: Licensed sales producer. Licensed staff who quote and sell.
- **CSR**: Customer service representative. Services the book; may or may not be licensed.
- **Producer**: Anyone whose job is writing new business.
- **P&C license**: Property and casualty license. Required to quote and sell auto, home, commercial.
- **L&H license**: Life and health license. Required to sell life and health products.
- **Book roll / acquisition**: Buying another agent's book of business.
- **E&O**: Errors and omissions insurance. The agency's own professional liability coverage.
