# AI Install Day 2 Guide: Make It Run

Four hours. By the end, your brain remembers on its own, a morning brief is scheduled, a weekly dashboard exists, you have built one skill of your own, and a weekly review keeps the whole thing current.

Start each step the same way as Day 1.

| Your route | What you type |
|---|---|
| Connected to AgencyBrain | `Use AgencyBrain to run AI Install step <step name>.` |
| Claude with the plugin | `/ai-install:<step name>` |
| Codex with the plugin | `$<step name>` |

## Open (15 minutes)

### Step: `resume`

The app reads yesterday's handoff note and tells you what is finished and what is next. Nothing is rebuilt. If Phase 6 did not happen yesterday, it happens now.

## Step: `phase-6-project` (25 minutes)

**Builds:** a second file in `current-projects/`

Run it again for your second real project. Two projects is enough for today. Add more later, one at a time.

## Step: `cleanup` (part of 45 minutes)

**What happens:** The app looks over the folder and lists anything out of place: duplicates, raw exports, anything that looks like a password. It proposes what to do with each. It moves or deletes nothing without your yes.

**You are done when:** the folder holds only what the brain should read.

## Step: `memory` (part of 45 minutes)

**Builds:** your master file, plus `memory/people/`, `memory/projects/`, and `memory/context/company.md`

**What happens:** The app turns what you built into a memory system and writes the master file that points to all of it. This is the file your AI app reads first in every new conversation. You also walk the glossary: cut terms you do not use, fix the ones that are wrong for your agency, add your own.

**You are done when:** every line in the master file points to a real file.

## Step: `cold-test` (15 minutes)

**What happens:** Open a brand new conversation. The app answers questions about you, your team, and your projects using only the folder. No hints from you.

**You are done when:** it gets them right cold. If it misses one, that is a gap in a file, and the app tells you which file.

## Break (10 minutes)

## Step: `brief-setup` (40 minutes)

**Builds:** `morning-brief/brief-instructions.md` and one scheduled task

**What happens:** The morning brief runs away from your computer, so it cannot open your folder. The app writes a short summary of your brain for the brief to carry, and you approve every line of it. Then it sets the schedule and runs the brief once, live, so you see a real one.

**You are done when:** you approved the summary, the test brief was useful, and the scheduled task exists at the time you chose.

**What the brief never does:** send, pay, sign, or delete anything. It reports. You decide.

**Plugin route:** the brief runs on your calendar and email. AgencyBrain numbers appear only if you are connected.

## Step: `dashboard` (25 minutes)

**Builds:** `dashboard/weekly-dashboard.html`

**What happens:** One page showing your projects and the number on each. Connected owners get the numbers filled in. On the plugin route you enter them.

**You are done when:** every number on the page is real and shows where it came from.

## Step: `build-skill` (35 minutes)

**Builds:** one skill folder with a `SKILL.md`

**What happens:** You pick one thing your agency does over and over. The app interviews you on how you do it, writes it down as a skill, and tests it on a real example.

**You are done when:** the skill produces the result on a real example without you explaining the process again.

## Step: `upkeep-setup` (15 minutes)

**Builds:** `upkeep/upkeep-instructions.md` and one scheduled task

**What happens:** Once a week, a review checks what changed and sends you a short numbered list of proposed updates, each with its evidence. It changes nothing. You approve changes later with `apply-updates`.

**You are done when:** the scheduled task exists and the test run proposed changes without making any.

## Close (15 minutes)

### Step: `check-day-2`

The app runs the full Day 2 check and tells you what passed. Fix anything open before the check-up call.

### Receipts (plugin route only)

Paste today's `AII-R1` lines into the receipt box in your portal.

## After the workshop

- **Tomorrow morning:** your first brief arrives.
- **Within a week:** your first upkeep list arrives.
- **When it does:** open your AI app on your computer with `MY BIZ BRAIN` connected and run `apply-updates`. It goes through the list one item at a time and changes only what you approve.
- **When you are ready to share with your team:** run `team-pack`. It builds a separate, team-safe folder and leaves your private files out. Your team never gets your brain.
