# AI Install Day 1 Guide: Build the Brain

Four hours. By the end, your `MY BIZ BRAIN` folder knows who you are, how you sound, what your rules are, who is on your team, and what you are working on.

Keep this open beside your AI app. You do not type prompts from this guide. You start a step, the app runs it, and you answer its questions.

## How to start any step

Every step has a short name. How you start it depends on your route.

| Your route | What you type |
|---|---|
| Connected to AgencyBrain | `Use AgencyBrain to run AI Install step <step name>.` |
| Claude with the plugin | `/ai-install:<step name>` |
| Codex with the plugin | `$<step name>` |

Example for the first step of the day: `Use AgencyBrain to run AI Install step phase-1-about-me.`

## The working rule

1. One step at a time. When a step finishes, stop and read what it made.
2. The app shows you every file before it saves. Correct it out loud. It is your brain, in your words.
3. If the app does not know something, it writes `Missing`. That is correct. It never fills a gap with a guess.
4. A step is done only when the file exists in your folder and the test passed. "Looks done" does not count.

## The four safety rules

These are written into your brain today and apply to everything it ever does.

1. Nothing sends to staff, clients, or prospects without your sign-off.
2. Never invent numbers. Real data only, or it says it does not have it.
3. Never touch pricing, commission, or comp.
4. Never confirm coverage, quote a rate, or bind anything in a draft.

## Before we start (15 minutes)

- Your AI app is open with `MY BIZ BRAIN` connected.
- `READY.txt` is in the folder from your pre-work.
- Voice input is on if you want to talk instead of type. Talking is faster for the interviews.

If the app cannot see the folder, reconnect `Documents > MY BIZ BRAIN` before anything else.

## Step: `phase-1-about-me` (30 minutes)

**Builds:** `about-me.md`

**What happens:** The app interviews you, one question at a time, about you, the agency, and what you are trying to do. Answer the way you would tell a new hire.

**You are done when:** you read the file and it sounds like you, with nothing invented, and the app's test question gets an answer only your file could give.

**If it goes wrong:** the file sounds like a resume. Tell the app "rewrite this in my words from my answers" and read it again.

## Step: `phase-2-voice` (40 minutes)

**Builds:** `brand-voice.md`

**What happens:** The app reads the writing samples you gathered and pulls out how you really write. Then it drafts a few lines in your voice and you grade them, line by line.

**You are done when:** you would send a draft it writes without rewriting the tone.

**If it goes wrong:** the voice is too polished. Give it rougher samples: real texts and real team emails beat anything you wrote for a newsletter.

## Step: `phase-3-rules` (35 minutes)

**Builds:** `working-preferences.md`

**What happens:** An interview about how you want the brain to work with you, with the four safety rules written in. You also decide what needs your approval. Anything that can send, pay, sign, or delete always asks you first.

**You are done when:** the four safety rules are in the file word for word, and the app refuses a test request that breaks one.

## Break (10 minutes)

## Step: `phase-4-content` (20 minutes)

**Builds:** `content-strategy.md`

**What happens:** A short interview about what you publish and why. If you do not run a content program, say so. The app writes a short "no program" version instead of making one up.

**You are done when:** the file matches what you actually do today.

## Step: `phase-5-team` (40 minutes)

**Builds:** `team-members.md`

**What happens:** The app walks your roster one person at a time: role, strengths, what they are working on, how you talk to them. Then it drafts a message to one team member as a test.

**You are done when:** the test message sounds like you talking to that person, and names no fact you did not give it.

**Keep in mind:** this file is private. It never goes in anything you hand to the team.

## Step: `phase-6-project` (30 minutes)

**Builds:** one file in `current-projects/`

**What happens:** You pick one real project. The app captures the goal, the one number that matters, the next milestone, and who owns what. If you are connected to AgencyBrain, it pulls that number for you. On the plugin route, you type it in.

**You are done when:** the number in the file is a real one, with its source and date.

**If we run short on time:** this step opens Day 2 instead.

## Close the day (20 minutes)

### Step: `handoff`

**Builds:** `SESSION-HANDOFF.md`, a short note of what is finished and what is next, so tomorrow starts where today stopped.

### Step: `check-day-1`

The app checks every Day 1 file and tells you what passed and what is still open. Fix anything it flags tonight or first thing tomorrow.

### Receipts (plugin route only)

Each step ends with one line that starts with `AII-R1`. Copy those lines into the receipt box in your AI Install portal. The line holds step names and file names only, never what is inside your files. If you are connected to AgencyBrain, skip this.
