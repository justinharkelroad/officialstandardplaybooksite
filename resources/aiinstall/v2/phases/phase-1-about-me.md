# Phase 1. About me

Outcome: you understand the real operator, business, history, current state, and direction behind the owner's work. This is not a LinkedIn bio. It is the version a sharp operating partner would need.

## What to do

1. Interview the owner one question at a time. The owner may be speaking instead of typing. Keep each question short, and repeat back what you heard in one sentence before moving on.
2. Cover, in this order:
   - Name and role.
   - How long they have been in insurance.
   - Carrier situation: captive or independent, and which carrier or appointments.
   - The book today: PIF count or premium range, and the mix of auto, home, life, and commercial.
   - Team size.
   - Their story: where they started, what they have built, what almost broke them.
   - What they are working toward over the next 1 to 3 years.
3. Write `about-me.md` in their words.
4. Show the file and ask what to correct. Save after approval.

## Test

After the file is saved, answer this from `about-me.md` only:

> What is the owner actually trying to build over the next 1 to 3 years, what could get in the way, and what do you still not know?

Use the owner's language. Do not fill gaps with assumptions. Ask the owner to grade the answer.

## Pass condition

- `about-me.md` exists in the folder.
- It includes the real carrier situation and book shape.
- It sounds like the owner, not like a professional biography.
- The test answer separates known facts from missing information, and the owner accepts it.

## If something goes wrong

- It reads like a resume: rewrite the sections the owner marks in the same plain language they used in the interview. Preserve facts. Add no claims. Show the changes before saving.
- The interview is running long: ask only what the file and its test require, and list the rest as follow-ups.
