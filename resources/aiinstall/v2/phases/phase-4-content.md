# Phase 4. Content strategy

Outcome: the workspace knows the owner's real content program. If they do not have one, that is recorded honestly. Build the content system they will actually use, not the one they wish they had.

## What to do

1. Ask first: "Do you post content today?" If the answer is no, go to step 4.
2. Ask one question at a time and cover:
   - The platforms they actually use.
   - Their audiences: prospects, clients, referral partners, recruits.
   - Their 3 to 5 core topics.
   - Their honest posting cadence.
   - Whether they are better on camera or in writing.
3. Write `content-strategy.md`. Keep it as short as their real program. If they are better on camera, add the repurposing frame: one weekly video becomes social posts, a client email, and follow-up snippets.
4. If they do not post today: write a short and truthful `content-strategy.md` that records that decision, what would cause them to revisit it, and which audience they would start with. Do not invent a posting cadence.
5. Show the file and ask what to correct. Save after approval.

## Test

Based only on `content-strategy.md` and `brand-voice.md`, propose the smallest content action the owner could realistically complete this week. Explain why it fits their actual cadence and preferred format. Do not create a large content plan.

## Pass condition

- `content-strategy.md` exists.
- It reflects what the owner actually does.
- It does not invent platforms or cadence.
- If the owner is camera-first, it includes a simple repurposing frame.
