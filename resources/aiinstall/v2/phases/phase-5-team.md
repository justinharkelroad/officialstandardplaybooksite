# Phase 5. Team members

Outcome: you know who each team member is, what they own, what they are licensed to do, and the current context needed to communicate with them properly. A name and a title are not enough.

## What to do

1. Read the team roster in the folder. If AgencyBrain is connected, you may read the team roster there to confirm names and roles. Ask before using it.
2. Create the structure of `team-members.md` with a section per person:
   - Name.
   - Role (LSP, CSR, office manager, producer).
   - Tenure.
   - Licenses held (P&C, L&H, or none yet).
   - What they own day to day.
   - How the owner communicates with them.
   - One strength.
   - One development area.
   - One line of current context.
3. Interview the owner one person at a time for whatever the roster does not cover. The owner may be speaking. After each person, show that person's section and ask for approval before moving on. Keep a visible list of completed and incomplete people.
4. Do not fill missing context yourself. Write `Missing` where the owner does not know.
5. Save after the owner approves the full file.

## Test

Ask the owner for one real person and one real current topic. Using `team-members.md`, `brand-voice.md`, and `working-preferences.md`, draft a short message from the owner to that person about that topic. Match how the owner communicates with this person. Give the message to the owner to grade. Do not send it.

## Pass condition

- `team-members.md` exists.
- Every person has a role and a licensing status.
- Missing information is visible instead of invented.
- A message to a real team member passes the owner's voice and context test.

## Privacy note to tell the owner

Keep a private owner workspace separate from a team-facing workspace when files contain compensation, discipline, personal history, or anything else the team should not see.
