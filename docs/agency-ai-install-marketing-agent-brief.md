## 3. What the Product Actually Is

This is not being positioned as an AI course, prompt pack, webinar, software subscription, or passive training library.

The product is a facilitated installation process. Buyers work inside a folder on their own computer called `MY BIZ BRAIN`. During the workshop, they turn their agency knowledge into organized files that Claude or Codex can use repeatedly.

The landing page’s central explanation is:

> The brain is not the chatbot. The brain is the files. The folder IS the brain.

In plain language, the buyer is creating a reusable operating context for AI. Instead of starting from zero in every chat, the AI has organized information about the owner, their voice, their preferences, their team, their projects, their memory, and the repeatable work they want it to perform.

The intended result is an AI system that understands the agency well enough to produce more relevant work, retain continuity across projects, and run repeatable workflows.

## 4. The Problem the Page Says It Solves

The page describes a buyer who:

- Already has ChatGPT or another AI tool open.
- Has watched demonstrations and saved prompts.
- Has told the team that the agency should use AI more.
- Still runs the agency primarily from information held in the owner’s head.
- Gets isolated answers from AI, but does not have a persistent system underneath those answers.
- Tries AI tips that disappear because the tool lacks business context and continuity.

The page’s diagnosis is:

> You do not have an AI problem. You have an install problem.

The enemy is not lack of access to AI. The enemy is fragmented experimentation without an organized, reusable system.

## 5. The Core Promise

The promise is that a qualified buyer who completes the pre-work and participates live will leave the two-day workshop with the core system built and working.

More specifically:

- Day One teaches the system who the owner is, how they communicate, how work should be done, who is on the team, and what the agency is working on.
- Day Two installs memory, repeatable skills, a scheduled task, and a live dashboard.
- Every phase is built inside the buyer’s own folder.
- The workshop uses live checkpoints so a participant who falls behind can be helped in the room.
- The buyer retains the folder and files after the workshop.
- The first automated morning brief is intended to run the morning after the build.

This is a build-with-you promise, not a done-for-you promise. The participant has to prepare, attend, and do the work during the sessions.

## 8. Exact Pre-Work

The pre-work is a required readiness gate, not an optional bonus.

The landing page says incomplete pre-work moves the person’s seat to a later date. It also says “No pre-work, no seat,” because the live workshop is reserved for building rather than fixing accounts and logins.

### Tool and account setup

The participant must:

1. Choose Claude or Codex.
2. Have an active account and be able to sign in.
3. Install and update the appropriate desktop app.
4. Use the same computer for pre-work and the live workshop.

Claude requirements:

- Install Claude Desktop.
- Use a Claude Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise plan because the required Code feature needs an eligible plan.
- Open Claude Desktop, choose Code, choose Local, and give it access to the workshop folder.

Codex requirements:

- Install the ChatGPT desktop app.
- Open Codex from the ChatGPT app and give it access to the workshop folder.
- A paid ChatGPT plan is recommended because a free account may hit usage limits during the workshop.

The landing page broadly states that the buyer needs their own eligible AI subscription, generally starting around $20 per month, billed separately by the AI provider and not included in the $997 workshop price.

### Folder setup

The participant creates this folder inside Documents:

`MY BIZ BRAIN`

They connect Claude or Codex to that folder and allow the desktop app to use it.

### Connection test

The participant asks the selected tool to create:

`READY.txt`

The file must contain:

`AI Install pre-work connection confirmed.`

This proves that the app is connected to the correct folder and can create files before the live build begins.

### Business information the participant gathers

The participant places the following inside the `MY BIZ BRAIN` folder:

1. **Five examples of their writing**
   - Emails.
   - Team messages.
   - Step-by-step instructions.
   - Social posts.
   - Anything else that genuinely sounds like the owner.

2. **A team list**
   - Each person’s name.
   - Job or role.
   - Main responsibilities.
   - Reporting relationship.
   - Important context the AI should understand about that person.

3. **A current-project list**
   - Project goal.
   - Owner.
   - Deadline.
   - What has already been completed.
   - What is blocking progress.

4. **Work preferences and rules**
   - Communication preferences.
   - Rules the team must follow.
   - Standards for how work should be done.
   - Things the owner repeatedly has to explain.

The material does not need to be polished. Notes, Word documents, PDFs, and pasted text are acceptable.

### Privacy cleanup

- Passwords.
- Secret access keys.
- Payment details.
- Social Security numbers.
- Policy numbers.
- Claim files.
- Private customer information.

### Six final readiness checks

Before Day One, the participant confirms:

1. The account and plan are active.
2. The desktop app is installed and updated.
3. The app can open the `MY BIZ BRAIN` folder.
4. The `READY.txt` test file exists.
5. The writing examples, team list, projects, and work preferences are in the folder.
6. Private and sensitive information has been removed.

The checklist saves progress in that browser’s local storage. It is not currently described as a centrally monitored completion system.

Do not say or imply:

- The buyer receives a fully done-for-you installation.
- The buyer does not need to participate live.
- The recordings are equivalent to attending.
- The buyer does not need a separate AI subscription.
- The system eliminates all future AI setup or maintenance.
- The AI autonomously runs the entire agency.
- The system is a proprietary chatbot or a Standard Playbook SaaS product.
- The system uploads all agency data to Justin.
- The workshop provides formal legal, compliance, cybersecurity, or data-privacy certification.
- The exact number of available seats.
- A refund or cancellation policy that has not been supplied.
- Specific pre-built skills that the page does not name.
- Testimonials, results, or performance statistics that are not verified.

Prefer “AI co-working brain,” “working system,” “organized business context,” “repeatable skills,” and “built live” over unexplained terms such as “source pack.”
