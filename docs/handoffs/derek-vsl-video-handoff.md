## The rules that keep all 9 feeling like one film

- Fonts: Anton for headlines and cards, always ALL CAPS. Inter for captions and
  lower thirds.
- Colors: ink #0a0a0b (near black), paper #f4f2ee (warm off white), blue #2997ff
  (the one accent). Put one blue word in the key frames and nowhere else.
- Section cards sit on an ink background, label in Anton caps with a blue
  underline bar. Hold about 0.8 seconds. These are the breath between parts.
- Captions are burned in the whole way through. Most people watch muted first.
- Transitions stay boring on purpose: hard cuts inside a section, one quick blue
  wipe (3 to 5 frames) only at the section breaks. No swishes, no 3D.
- Text lives in the lower third or on a card, never across Justin's face.
- Music builds through each section, peaks on the punch line, pulls back and
  goes quiet under the personal and emotional lines.
- Hard rule on all on-screen copy: no dashes of any kind. Use periods and
  commas.
