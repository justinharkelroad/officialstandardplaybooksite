# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Standard Playbook members use the authenticated app to complete daily and weekly coaching work, review progress, run guided AI coaching Flows, set goals, and generate personal audio or documents. They use the product on desktop browsers and on iPhone and Android phones, often in focused but time-limited sessions.

## Product Purpose

The member app turns the Standard Playbook coaching system into a repeatable personal practice. Success means members can move through Core 4, Playbook, Flows, Debrief, Missions, Targets, and 90 Day Audio without losing context, regardless of device.

## Brand Personality

Direct, disciplined, and editorial. The interface should feel like the existing Standard Playbook identity: confident typography, squared geometry, restrained surfaces, and a precise blue accent.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product look like a generic rounded SaaS dashboard, a decorative wellness app, or a scaled-down desktop page. Avoid ornamental gradients, excessive cards, soft glass effects, and mobile layouts that require horizontal scrolling or hide essential actions.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the member's next action clear and reachable.
2. Preserve the established Standard Playbook identity across web and native surfaces.
3. Adapt structure for touch and narrow screens instead of shrinking desktop layouts.
4. Keep coaching content readable and protect member data across sessions and accounts.
5. Prefer familiar controls and predictable navigation over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Support reduced motion, visible keyboard focus, 44 by 44 pixel touch targets, safe areas, light and dark modes, and large-text sanity checks. Core functionality must remain usable from 320 CSS pixels wide and must not require horizontal scrolling.
